the `comfyui` skill distributed with
[NousResearch/hermes-agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent).

`kshitijk4poor`, `alt-glitch`, and `purzbeats` as contributors to the skill.

workflow batch runner, additional download hardening, bilingual documentation,
